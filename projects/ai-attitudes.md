## The question

*The A.I. Dilemma*, a 2023 talk by the Center for Humane Technology, warns about the risks of rapidly deploying AI, and its comment section became a large, unprompted record of how people reacted. Our team wanted to understand public sentiment about AI risk: how people feel, what they fear, and what they think should be done. Unlike survey responses, these comments were voluntary first reactions, which made them a useful window into how people actually respond to the topic.

## Research methods

Our dataset contained 5,854 comments. We drew a sample of 45, pulling from the beginning, middle, and end of the comment section at irregular intervals so no single part of the conversation dominated. We coded each comment for its key sentiments and ideas, then compared codes across the sample to identify recurring themes.

To check our work, we gave the full dataset to ChatGPT and asked it to identify the most frequent themes, themes that appeared together, unusual outliers, and anything we might have missed. It independently surfaced several of our themes, which increased our confidence in them, and suggested four new ones. We kept the two we felt were most worth exploring.

## Findings

We identified seven themes:

- **Disbelief and cognitive overwhelm:** awe and struggle to grasp how quickly AI is developing
- **Anxiety and powerlessness:** urgency and a sense of having no control over what comes next
- **Call to action:** demands for regulation, safeguards, and wider public awareness
- **AI utopianism and dystopianism:** the tension between AI as a solution to society's problems and AI as a path to chaos
- **Praise:** gratitude to the presenters for raising the issue
- **AI as existential risk** *(identified by ChatGPT)*: fear that AI could threaten human survival
- **Human nature and misuse** *(identified by ChatGPT)*: the view that the real danger is AI in the wrong hands

The most common sentiments were praise, concern, fear, disbelief, and pessimism, and gratitude for the talk often gave way to fear. Overall, commenters recognized AI's potential benefits but feared its risks, and they shared a desire for accountability, transparency, and caution in how AI is developed.

## Reflection

Combining the two approaches showed us what each does well. Manual coding let us read tone, sarcasm, and implied meaning, which ChatGPT sometimes misread, for example by taking a sarcastic comment as sincere. ChatGPT let us see patterns across all 5,854 comments in a fraction of the time hand-coding would take. Used together, each one checked the other.

I also learned to think carefully about where data comes from. YouTube comments are short, surface-level reactions, and the people leaving them are likely already interested in the topic. The video's focus on risk probably amplified the fear we saw. Those limits don't make the data less useful, but they shape what conclusions it can support.
