Passionate About Human-Centered Data & Technology.

I am an Information Science student at the University of Colorado Boulder with a minor in Philosophy. My work centers around human-technology interaction: how we can design for organizations to better manage data and improve how the people within them experience it. My emphasis in ethics has shaped how I approach technology and data analytics, encouraging me to consider how data should be collected, used, and governed in ways that respect the people behind it.
